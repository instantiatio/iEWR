# iEWR 7.0.6 Eternal Alpha status

Research release prepared from published iEWR 7.0.5. MP base-v3.16 contains 37 candidate descriptions: 25 have new description editions and 12 retain their exact bodies. Human authorized GitHub publication as Latest, updating main, and local activation on 5 November 2026. Publication does not expand the evidence or source rights described below. The accepted 7.0.5 and exact prepublication 7.0.6 remain preserved.

The 18 established DPF families were reconciled against pinned author sources. Ten author files changed, six are byte-identical to their author predecessors, and two historical standalone sources have established successor routes without a same-named file in the checked current tree. [Source reconciliation](docs/SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md) records the lineage and source boundary; [ORIGINS.json](ORIGINS.json) retains exact current and prior provenance. New upstream refinements, earlier adaptation losses and an additional English translation distortion are distinguished. WEP's existing original-comparison rule is retained and made more explicit for successive revisions.
