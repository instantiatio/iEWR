# Applications

Use iEWR to build apps, websites, and automation, investigate questions, prepare decisions, or organize the work needed to change a system. That system can be an application, a technical product, an organization, a process, or a method.

The directions below describe uses and possible results. [Cases & Experiments](/iEWR/eng/cases/) records work actually performed and the scope of its checks. You can begin with an ordinary question in [Try iEWR](/iEWR/eng/try/); choosing a direction here is optional.

## Apps, websites, and automation {#software}

**An assignment:** “Create a working application or website for this idea. Implement the code, interface, and data it needs, check the result, and prepare the run instructions.”

This includes new software, development of an existing product, websites, APIs, scripts, and workflow automation. Depending on the task, the work covers implementation design, coding, interfaces, tests, debugging, documentation, and preparation for deployment.

The [GEO CRM case](/iEWR/eng/cases/geo-crm/) shows a connected application developed through successive assignments. [Try iEWR](/iEWR/eng/try/) includes a checked coding experiment with explicit expected results.

## Research lab

**An assignment:** “Help me investigate this question. Review the sources, identify testable hypotheses, and propose a way to distinguish them.”

Results can include a source review, an experiment plan, a computational study, data analysis, and conclusions tied to observations. iEWR connects the question, method, evidence, and unresolved issues. Physical experiments also need the relevant equipment and participants.

## Decision engineering

**An assignment:** “Compare these options against our goals and constraints. Explain the consequences and prepare a basis for choosing.”

The work can produce alternatives, criteria, calculations, scenarios, and a recommendation with its grounds. The person retains significant trade-offs and the decision. Clarifying what is known, what remains uncertain, and what would change the choice is part of the result.

## AI-native engineering team

**An assignment:** “Help organize this work across people and AI, with clear contributions and a result we can check together.”

iEWR supports agreement on responsibilities, delegated decisions, interfaces, delivery, acceptance, and continuation. The team may start with one person and one agent. Additional performers and coordination tools are selected for the task and the capabilities of the agent environment.

## Organizational engineering

**An assignment:** “Design the transfer of product support to another team. Work out the roles, interactions, access, knowledge, and readiness criteria.”

Results can include process and responsibility models, interfaces between teams, a pilot plan, and checks of the proposed arrangement. People authorized within the organization carry out the changes and assess their effects. The engineering result connects the intended way of working to what can be put into practice.

## Method factory

**An assignment:** “Find a suitable way to solve this problem. Adapt an existing method or develop what is missing, check it on the task, and preserve what is useful.”

The initial MethodPack is a starting point. The agent can investigate professional sources, including the internet through available tools, apply FPF, and obtain the domain detail it needs. DMG covers finding, reusing, adapting, and constructing methods. Repeatedly useful methods are retained with their grounds and conditions.

The [GEO CRM case](/iEWR/eng/cases/geo-crm/) includes the development and application of LIQ-13 in that project. [FPF, DMG & MethodPack](/iEWR/eng/foundations/methods/) explains the methodological basis.

## Self-developing engineering environment

**An assignment:** “Investigate this difficulty in iEWR's own behavior, improve the way it works, and check the change.”

iEWR can be the system under investigation: its instructions, navigation, methods, architecture, or supporting tools. You set the improvement goal and scope; the agent researches, changes, and checks the environment using its engineering means. Development of a project method can also happen during the assigned work without revising the whole environment.

See the [7.0.6 self-development procedure](https://github.com/instantiatio/iEWR/blob/7.0.6/docs/SELF_DEVELOPMENT.md) for the operational details.
