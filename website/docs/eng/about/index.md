# About Instantiatio

Instantiatio is a research programme on AI-native-first ways of working: people determine goals and value, while AI independently organizes and performs engineering work within an assignment.

The programme connects the [nine AI-native-first principles](/iEWR/eng/foundations/ai-native-first/), [System Formation](/iEWR/eng/foundations/system-formation/), and [Engineering Work Runtime](/iEWR/eng/foundations/ewr/). It investigates how these ideas expand the systems that people and small teams can create, with attention to useful results and the total effort of obtaining them.

## iEWR in practice

iEWR is the programme's working engineering environment. Its instructions, methodology, methods, and project resources enable an agent to determine the necessary work, perform it, check results, and continue across sessions.

This site describes **iEWR 7.0.6 Eternal Alpha**. [Try iEWR](/iEWR/eng/try/) is the practical entry; [Cases & Experiments](/iEWR/eng/cases/) connects work to results and checks. Foundations provides optional full conceptual materials.
