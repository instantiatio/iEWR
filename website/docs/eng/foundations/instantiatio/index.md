# Instantiatio

Instantiatio is a research programme exploring how human intent becomes a working result through models, activity, and verification. AI-native-first is its central idea: people define what is worth doing, while AI takes on the organization and execution of engineering work.

The name also describes the process being studied: turning an intention into something that exists and works. A useful outcome may be an application, an engineering design, a document, an organization, or a change to an existing system.

## From intent to an assignment

Between an idea and a result lies engineering work. Someone must understand the situation, establish requirements, choose methods, connect contributions, perform operations, and check what happened. AI can carry out a connected part of that work, including deciding which operations are needed next.

An intention is more than a prompt. It includes a desired change, its value, the conditions of use, constraints, and signs of success. “Design a bicycle” means different things for a city commuter and a mountain rider, a child and an adult, a custom build and a manufactured product. These distinctions determine both the design and how it can be checked.

The initial intention need not be complete. A person and AI can clarify it together. The important connection is between the assignment and the result that would actually serve its purpose.

## Keep the whole system in view

Independent execution requires a connected account of the result: what should exist, how its parts relate, which decisions apply, and what still needs to be established. This account can be distributed across requirements, models, code, calculations, decisions, and test results.

Its value lies in the connections. A requirement should explain why a design decision matters; a check should establish a property needed in use. Otherwise, independently correct components can combine into a system that fails its purpose.

The necessary detail depends on the decision. A small change may need a few clear conditions; a complex integration needs a richer model. The aim is enough shared context to choose and check the work.

## Organize the path as well as the operations

A conventional tool performs an operation selected by its user. An AI agent can also investigate which operation is needed, select a method, use a tool, interpret the outcome, and change its next action.

This makes part of the organization of work available for delegation. The person can assign a result rather than manually route every intermediate output between tools and specialists. Plans remain useful, but new evidence can change them.

Intent, a model of the result, action, observation, and correction form a continuing relationship. A failed check may change the implementation, the method, or an assumption about the desired system. An already sufficient result may remove the need for further work.

## Execution depends on the world being changed

Software offers short feedback loops: code can be changed, executed, and tested within a digital environment. Physical engineering adds manufacturing, materials, equipment, measurement, and testing. AI can help connect these contributions, while the physical work retains its own costs and timescales.

The same distinction applies to organizations. Preparing a new operating model is different from people adopting it. The useful result and the means of obtaining evidence must match the system being changed.

## Human judgment and responsibility

People determine goals, assess value, set delegation bounds, and accept results. AI can investigate alternatives and consequences, but the ability to propose a choice does not grant authority to make it.

As execution becomes easier, choosing which possibilities deserve attention becomes more consequential. Quality includes the value of the result, the consequences of using it, and the total effort of obtaining and maintaining it.

## How the ideas connect

- [AI-native-first](/iEWR/eng/foundations/ai-native-first/) provides principles for organizing activity around AI capabilities.
- [System Formation](/iEWR/eng/foundations/system-formation/) connects the work to the purpose and current state of the system.
- [Engineering Work Runtime](/iEWR/eng/foundations/ewr/) describes the supporting engineering environment.
- **iEWR** is the working environment in which AI organizes and performs assigned engineering work using these ideas.

The programme investigates which new possibilities this organization makes practical and under what conditions it produces useful results.
