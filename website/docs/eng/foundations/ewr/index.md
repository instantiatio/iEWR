# Engineering Work Runtime

**Engineering Work Runtime (EWR) is a concept for an engineering environment that organizes and supports work by people and AI, from intent to a checked result and its continuation.**

It brings together the AI performer, professional methods, execution tools, and project resources. iEWR is a working environment of this kind: AI independently organizes and performs engineering work assigned by a person.

## What the environment contributes

An agent can investigate a problem, write code, perform calculations, and use tools. Engineering work also needs connections between the purpose, decisions, methods, authorized actions, actual results, and further work.

For example, an agent may change a program and pass its tests. Whether the change is suitable for use also depends on which properties were checked, what remains unknown, and who may accept or deploy it. EWR keeps these relationships part of the work.

The primary user has an intention and domain understanding: enough to judge the value of a result and its fitness for use. They may be an engineer, researcher, entrepreneur, or manager. The environment helps them obtain contributions outside their own execution specialty.

## Four connected contributions

| Contribution | What it provides |
|---|---|
| AI performer | Investigates the situation, selects methods, organizes and performs the assignment |
| Professional methods | Ways of obtaining results, their conditions, and suitable checks |
| Agent execution environment (ADE) | Files, commands, computation, programming, data, and connected services |
| Project resources | Sources, models, decisions, results, evidence, and grounds for continuation |

The person sets the assignment, value, and delegation bounds. EWR connects these contributions in the actual working configuration. Existing agents and tools may provide much of the necessary support; extra mechanisms are added when they supply a needed property.

In iEWR 7.0.6, the methodological combination is **Instructions + FPF + MethodPack**. [DMG](/iEWR/eng/foundations/methods/) obtains a suitable way of working through search, reuse, adaptation, or construction. Domain frameworks, professional literature, standards, and skills can supply useful methods.

## Work takes shape during execution

Runtime means that the necessary work can be determined and reorganized while it is performed. The agent uses the assignment and current system state to choose the next contribution. Results and new information can change methods, sequence, or the question being investigated.

[System Formation](/iEWR/eng/foundations/system-formation/) explains this choice in relation to the system. EWR provides the environment for organizing and performing it.

A clear, authorized action can proceed directly. Several dependent contributions may need a plan. A sufficient existing result can be reused. These are responses to the task rather than universal stages.

## Independent work within an assignment

The mandate consists of the task, allowed actions, constraints, and authority. A person can work closely with AI or delegate substantial work. The agent makes ordinary execution decisions within that scope and returns questions that require an undelegated choice.

Capability, permission, and result fitness are distinct. Access to a tool supplies a technical means. Methods help the performer make and check decisions. Where a boundary must hold independently of an agent's judgment, the execution environment must supply the corresponding technical protection.

## Continue beyond one session

Projects outlast individual conversations and tools. Continuation requires accessible grounds: the current assignment, accepted decisions, source and method editions, completed work, checking results, and open questions.

The next performer recovers what is needed rather than replaying the whole history. If an external operation's reply was lost, its effect is established before it is repeated. A record of uncertainty is part of the state to be continued.

Methods and the supporting environment can also be improved through an assigned change. Previous editions and relevant checking grounds remain available. The purpose is to support the project rather than continually rebuild its support system.

## Place in the programme

Instantiatio studies the realization of intent. AI-native-first supplies principles for organizing activity. System Formation connects the system to the necessary work. EWR describes the supporting environment; iEWR provides its practical implementation.

Together they motivate the question of which systems a person or small team can now create, and at what total cost and quality.
