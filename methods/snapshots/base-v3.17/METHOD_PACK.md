A query translation, synonym, or hypothetical answer remains a hypothesis, not a fact. If the needed result changes after reading, explain the change and preserve the unresolved initial difficulty. F.1:4.4 supplies this general inquiry; here state is linked to executed routes, queries, source units, editions, and covered scope so the next reader does not repeat a rejected interpretation.
2. For repeated access, assess the workload: corpus growth, query and update frequencies, languages and formats, questions with known names and unknown vocabulary, historical and current uses, latency, authorized data flows, and the recipient's preparation. Compare direct dossier reading, exact and lexical search, persistent complementary representations, and deferred original checking for the same needed result and horizon. Do not require lexical search to fail before a semantic route when the workload already makes it a serious option. Hard permission and capability constraints exclude infeasible choices before cost ranking.
3. If a prepared route is needed, index original bodies through bounded units, including useful prefaces, bibliographies, examples, and notes; headings, cards, and summaries are supplementary fields. The exact lexical channel preserves punctuation-sensitive identifiers. The dense-vector channel records compatibility of document and query encoders, dimension, normalization, and comparison convention; equal dimension does not establish compatibility. A structural route retains source-grounded inclusion, definitions, references, and edition succession without declaring a mention graph to be method dependencies. A multilevel summary retains source membership and an independent return to leaf units. For an approximate nearest-neighbor search route, select a manageable representative subset: obtain exact neighbors in the same vector space, with the same corpus and query encodings and similarity convention, and compare them with the approximate result. Record the subset, settings, neighbor-return recall, and losses; this checks algorithmic approximation, not semantic relevance. Separately check source-based judgments: did the encoder preserve needed distinctions of negation, condition, and rare identifier? High ANN recall does not repair meaning lost in encoding. Select only arrangements whose construction, updating, and reading are justified by the workload.
4. Every search result returns identity, edition and fragment, route, query and filters, a score with its local meaning, and an operation for opening the source. Permission restrictions apply before unauthorized disclosure. Apply a governing-authoritative-basis filter (`current-authority`) only to a use where the owner has established it; a historical question retains old editions. Do not make an assumed topic or department the sole admission condition for every route. With filtering after top-K, acknowledge recall loss; compare over-retrieval or integrated filtering without violating the data boundary.
5. Select the next route by the missing contribution: exact ID; source vocabulary, translation, or dense vectors; expansion to the parent section or definition; independent checking of the original when a shared blind spot is suspected. Merge identities of results and overlapping fragments, retain editions and contradictory claims separately, and preserve route provenance. Raw lexical and vector scores are not common probabilities. Rank fusion may be used, for example the sum of `1/(c+rank)` with a declared positive c; select c and depths on development questions. Check allocation of room to routes' unique candidates before shortlisting: if both routes find A/B, a unique D from the semantic channel must not disappear merely because A/B occur repeatedly. Uniqueness is a reason to check, not evidence of truth.
6. For an independent pass through the original, enumerate authorized units of the selected snapshot without a relevance or category filter, and divide them by source order or declared strata/sample into bounded batches with addresses and context. A batch may return several plausible contributions, none, and unknowns. Preserve processed and unprocessed areas; expand promising fragments into reading units. Do not select batches using the same classifier whose blind spot is being checked. Local batch probabilities are not a global rank; an item-level criterion or jointly rescored finalists are needed separately. Even viewing all content does not guarantee recognition of a relation between blocks. A supplier without enumeration limits the promised independent pass to the set that can actually be opened.
7. Open the decisive source context. For an arbitrary contribution, compare the source claim, operation, input, result, and conditions with the case and domain criterion; distinguish support, refutation, a missing fact, and conflict. For a Method, apply A.3.1.QM; for candidate FPF use, E.11.PUR; interpretation, grounds, and permission do not follow from a score. Allow a useful explanation or question and the unsuitability of all candidates. If ranking winner A fails sufficiency, another B's maximum score does not qualify A. Link the returned candidate, criterion, evidence fragments, and threshold. Separate four checks: ranking is candidate order; calibration is correspondence of stated probabilities to the observed evaluation event in this population; threshold classification is errors and abstentions under the selected action rule; logical coherence is consistency of answers to logically related questions. Success in one check does not establish the others. Select the threshold on development cases with the cost of missed or wrong action, then check on separate control cases unused in threshold development, including close but unsuitable cases, missing facts, a set where every candidate is unsuitable, material negation, and long irrelevant input. Preserve the model and version, prompt, question and criterion, option order and wording, preprocessing, language, source scope, action cost, and threshold setting. Cheap selection for reading and a consequential recommendation or interruption may have different thresholds. When these conditions change, reopen the affected qualification; a score used only for ordering retains precisely that limited meaning. Retrieved instructions remain source data.
8. If a whole result is needed, use C.39:4.2–4.6 for backward supplier search, joins of meanings, units, time, source, and case, and forward continuation. On the corpus side, retain a bounded frontier of partial constructions: selected contributions with source-confirmed suitability, obtained results separately from possible suppliers, missing inputs, joins, failed prerequisites, editions, and permissible resources. Add a new supplier only where it actually closes a missing result. Discard a demonstrated contradiction or justified dominance for the same result and conditions; mark forced budget pruning as approximation. Check total shared resource capacity and precedence, not just pairs. If a source gives a quantity and the next step requires an ID, find an ID supplier or transformation, or return a join gap. Another search result does not repair unexplained permission or capability.
9. For an overview, first distinguish an illustrative example, thematic overview, and frequency claim. Define population membership, editions and unit, rules for repeated messages, and what “main” means. Use the inventory to allocate reading batches with a common question and coding rule; a long unit retains identity across parts. Link a claim to exact passages, entity, time, condition, and underlying set of source units; distinguish the source's position from the reader's conclusion. Merge compatible paraphrases by meaning, taking the union of source sets; do not merge contradictory, conditional, and minority evidence into majority wording. Parent, child, and overlapping summaries do not add population members. For counts, each eligible unit is counted once per predicate; make topic overlap and the unread or unknown part of the denominator explicit. Do not divide relevance-selected results by the whole population as a prevalence estimate. A broader estimate requires a separately justified sampling method. Words such as “all,” “most,” and “typical” require this basis.
10. Compare the next affordable check with an already sufficient result: what will the missing contribution change, and what do reading, delay, and application cost? Reserve final closure back to the source and KCA-03 explanation. Stop at a sufficient recipient result, a hard budget or channel limit, or no reachable useful change. The return distinguishes sufficient material, rejected candidates, a missing case fact, an access limit, and budget-limited partiality. Absence of findings supports a conclusion only relative to the scope and route. A changed question or criterion may invalidate earlier negative selection; do not hide a unit permanently.

**Compare or repair the arrangement.** For a material choice, compare credible arrangements at the same required result and quality, including the incumbent with its current limits when one exists and an existing or simpler arrangement when it could meet the requirement. Use independent work episodes with unfamiliar vocabulary, a remote exception, cases where use is naturally unnecessary, a source change, and workload languages. Do not derive all questions from the same summaries used for searching; develop thresholds and features separately from the control comparison on cases excluded from development. The reference set may be incomplete: pool candidates from several serious routes, obtain source-based judgments from a suitable reader, and, where possible, sample outside the set. Material outside the set or without an assessment remains unassessed or unknown, not irrelevant; uncertainty in the judgment set limits conclusions about completeness and coverage. Matching one preferred answer string does not replace sufficiency and conditions for admissible alternatives. Observe the complete useful result, unsupported advice or a missed condition, and full costs: preparation, extraction repair, storage and updating, all model calls and inputs, latency and its tails, primary-context load, human reading, interruption, application, and recovery. Estimates remain estimates; remaining context window is separate from total paid tokens. For diagnosis, manually supply sufficient candidates while keeping the evaluator and delivery unchanged; then fix the set and change the evaluator, fix a contribution with established suitability and change delivery, fix the source and query and change the route. After repairing a component, repeat the affected whole use: help from an ideal external supplier does not establish natural end-to-end effectiveness. Accept, preserve, narrow, or stop according to that use, not the size of the union or a tidy typed output. Retain the incumbent when it still meets the receiving requirements and the proposed change does not earn its construction and maintenance burden. If it fails a necessary requirement, compare an attainable repair, another arrangement, or a bounded unsupported-use return; cheapness alone does not justify retaining it. A missing source contribution calls for obtaining that contribution, while a finding, assessment, or delivery failure calls for repairing the corresponding operation. A harmless sufficient search does not require a new large study.

**Output and join.** A bounded contribution with source addresses, partial synthesis or a precise gap, checked scope and editions, material counterevidence, the next operation, and stopping. KCA-03 receives sufficient claims, conditions, and joins rather than the full dump of findings. A source change returns affected state through KCA-04; a reusable conditional result goes to KCA-05 only for future use.

**Example.** Eight current messages correspond to twelve files and two overlapping summaries. The latest five messages contain a direct objection about effort; one more permits effort with a working template, and one warns about disclosure. The result 5/8 is an observed count, not consensus or an estimate for the entire audience. An authorized replacement withdraws one objection: its coding and the count 4/8 are reopened; the rare disclosure concern remains. Duplicating a summary or an old edition does not change the denominator.

**Basis and limit.** KCAE.USE/INDEX/SEARCH/ASSESS/COMPOSE/EVAL; exact source locations are in the index and provenance information. FPF F.1:4.2/4.4, C.39:4.2–4.6, C.11.DUA:4.1–4.4, C.2.8:4, E.11.PUR:4.1–4.3, A.3.1.QM:4, and A.10.1:4. New local operations are routes, bounded scanning and synthesis, partial-construction state, and comparisons for this corpus. No exhaustive semantic coverage, optimal retrieval, or empirical superiority is claimed.

**Worked application and changed conditions.** A complete constructed case. The retention period of X-17 for batch 62 is needed. A summary says “5 days,” exact search finds §4, and independent viewing of an adjacent table finds the note “batch 62: 30 days.” Open §4 and the note in the same edition; retain the summary as a derived aid, with the note as the source of the condition. Output to the reader: “30 days for batch 62,” exact address, and condition; a sufficient result stops search without a new index. Variation: the batch number is unknown. Return two conditional branches and a question about the batch; another high relevance score does not establish the missing fact. This illustrates bounded direct and lexical reading and reading of the original, not an observation of ANN, calibration, or a complete overview.

**Experience and checking limits.** Examples are constructed. The description has candidate status; actual suitability and results are checked for the specific inputs, performer, and conditions of application.

### KCA-03 v1.5 — Deliver sufficient reading in the actual execution environment

**When.** Useful material has been found but needs to be delivered to a person, model, tool, or subsequent reader without losing conditions and at an acceptable cost. **Input:** the recipient's action and the reader, a contribution with checked suitability, source dependencies, the actual execution environment, and constraints.

**Basis and local addition.** FPF C.2.8 and C.39:4.5 define a sufficient explanation for this reader; C.11.DUA and F.1 define the usefulness of further reading. Added here are execution-environment operations: edition-bound chunking, accounting for actually returned chunks and truncation, reserving capacity for complete connected source reading and the answer, and checking delivery capability and authorized data flows. They are needed because the existence of a complete file does not mean that a person or model received a material condition.

**Method.**
1. Determine what the reader must do: select a source, understand a distinction, execute a method, check a conclusion, or support a service. Clarify only use-changing preparation, tools and access, current instructions, available time, remaining context, and output limit. A discovery package may be short: identity, edition, relevance, and address; application requires actual recognition features, inputs, operations, exceptions, stopping, and joins. Do not impose a universal token limit for every use.
2. From the selected fragment, expand connected reading to cover the definition, adjacent exception, table or footnote, prerequisite, and external criterion or release note that change interpretation or action. Distinguish source fragments from an author's explanation or translation. Expansion ends when the reader can obtain the needed result without materially filling gaps; unrelated background is unnecessary. A missing material source returns a dependent limitation, not an invented complete method.
3. When chunking, pin the document, edition, and unit; track actually returned chunks, the next cursor or position, and completion. Check for gaps, duplicates, and generation changes before combining. If output is truncated, request a smaller chunk and check its boundary; a successful exit code is not the end of the unit. A saved file's hash attests to the file's bytes, not that the reader saw them in tool output. Very long reading permits intermediate notes with source addresses and selective rereading; notes also lose information and retain an exact return to the source.
4. Select an actual delivery capability. Direct reading is for a manageable set of materials; selective reading follows the recipient's needs; an isolated reader requires an actual separate context with sufficient returned grounds; controlled history recovery requires an execution-environment operation. “Forget” does not delete history or enlarge the window; a skill or tool with a short result does not establish isolation. Check the property against available execution-environment documentation and a material observable test; without grounds, do not rely on it in the construction. A new reader explicitly receives its own active instructions, question, permissions, and needed previous results; a shared filesystem and credentials do not become an isolated environment because the context is different.
5. Before searching, reserve capacity for complete reading of the selected source, joins, the answer and uncertainty, and interface overhead. Count total calls, model input, cached input, and free space in the main context separately. Observation of remaining capacity through execution-environment means is preferable; without it, call an estimate an estimate. If 12000 remains, complete reading for use and an answer require 7000, and a candidate dump requires 9000, reduce the dump, use an actually available external reader, or narrow the promised result rather than presenting it as feasible delivery.
6. At every transfer, check authorized material, destination, storage, and logging. A public-text route may receive an abstract query while private case facts remain local. Sanitization for authorized transfer must not erase the decisive distinction; if it does, an approved local route or a return stating lack of authority is needed. Account for the external reader or model's cost even if the main context has shrunk.
7. Deliver the result for the reader with grounds, conditions, and next action; for execution, include necessary operations and joins; for subsequent use, include edition and dependencies. Distinguish API delivery, UI display, understanding, and actual application. If use requires receipt, observe the relevant boundary; unknown receipt remains unknown. If the decisive distinction is not recovered, repair expression, preparation, or access rather than adding unrelated text.

**Output, check, and stop.** The reader has and can obtain sufficient connected reading in the selected edition within the permissible budget. Check with a question that depends on the vulnerable distinction, preserving the initial answer before a hint; recognizing a name does not test action. If a sufficient source is already available and understood, no separate package is needed. Changed execution-environment limits, model, reader, or source reopen the affected delivery choice. An available explanatory probe is not evidence of human mastery or sustained empirical performance.

**Example.** A passage says “automatic resend.” The complete unit and footnote require protocol v2 and absence of receipt. An analyst receives a conditional branch and a question about the missing protocol; an integration engineer additionally receives ID mapping and the source-generation contract. The same passage is insufficient for both. Truncation before the footnote requires further reading even if the whole file was saved.

**Basis and limit.** KCAE.DELIVER:4.1–4.6, SOURCE:4.4; FPF C.2.8:4.1–4.5, C.11.DUA:4, F.1:4.4.4–4.4.5, C.39:4.5. Execution-environment operations, data flows, and actual receipt are supplied by the installation; text does not create a missing capability.

**Worked application and changed conditions.** A complete constructed case. The recipient must choose an action under an “automatic resend” rule. Inputs are the original paragraph and footnote “only protocol v2 and absence of receipt”; the case specifies v1. Deliver the rule together with the footnote, edition, and case mismatch; a useful output is to withhold automatic retry and obtain a compatible method. The check requires recovery of both prerequisites from the text actually delivered. If the tool truncated the footnote, finish reading a smaller chunk before deciding; if the protocol is unknown, return the precise question rather than permission. This is not an observation of a remote effect or human understanding.

**Experience and checking limits.** Examples are constructed. The description has candidate status; actual suitability and results are checked for the specific inputs, performer, and conditions of application.

### KCA-04 v1.5 — Preserve coherent editions of sources and representations

**When.** A source, extraction, permissions, model, or case grounds change while reading and reuse continue. A fixed dossier for one-time use needs only the fixed basis of KCA-01. **Input:** source inventory, used dependency slices, actual storage and reading contracts, and authority and retention rules.

**Basis and local addition.** FPF A.10.1 determines a conclusion's dependence on changed grounds; G.11 determines the update scope; A.6.3.RT and F.1 supply preservation and reading. The local addition is an inventory of sources and representations, coherent generations, saving and reloading, pointer switching with actual protection, preservation of old reading, an exact or limited path, and handling deletion and access revocation. These are conditional storage constructions, not guarantees created by the method's text.

**Method.**
1. Distinguish addition, deletion, content change, relocation, and change of role or authority. Obtain a stable snapshot, edition, or export, or detect concurrent change and repeat affected retrieval. For the actual change channel, check the feed, subscription, or polling and the latest successful observation. No events from a broken channel mean unknown currentness; reconcile the current inventory, providing for gap reconciliation when reusing a feed.
2. For each derived or used result, store only the dependencies it relies on: its own bytes, parent heading, table, note or definition, source of a generated prefix, extraction, model, preprocessing and comparison versions; assessment adds the case, criterion, and source role; recommendation adds workload and capability. Under A.10.1, compare the change at claim scale and the actual dependency of the recipient's result use; a mention, backlink, and hash do not establish dependency. G.11 supplies scope and update selection; the direct domain method supplies the recheck conclusion. An unchanged byte block under an “obsolete” heading requires reconsideration of context-dependent use.
3. For a service, assemble a candidate generation with a stable ID and manifest: source membership and editions, configurations, and the ready/unavailable/limited scope of each representation. Update changed and context-dependent units and references, and mark removed members; embedding reuse is permissible only while source, context, and space compatibility are preserved. The exact path may be ready when an optional vector path fails. Failure of a necessary source-reading means withholds the corresponding use.
4. Save the candidate, close and reload it; check the inventory, addresses, search→open and pagination, a changed case, and an unchanged control. Compare incremental assembly with rebuilding on stable input: for deterministic results, compare exact values and membership; for stochastic results, compare promised semantic and retrieval properties and variation. Task completion does not establish equivalence.
5. Select an actual publication arrangement. One store can provide the needed transaction and isolation only within its own scope. For several stores, immutable objects or namespaces may be constructed for each generation: durably save all necessary objects, check address resolution, then atomically switch a small catalog pointer to the manifest. Readers take the pointer once and pass the generation to search, open, and pages. This requires actually immutable names, durability, an atomic pointer operation, and object retention during reading. A manifest alone does not create a distributed transaction.
6. For concurrent publication, use the expected previous pointer value and CAS or an actual equivalent; the losing writer reconciles grounds rather than installing old over new. Retain old objects for as long as a read lease or reference count requires, or until a declared retention boundary; return expiry explicitly. If these guarantees are absent, choose a maintenance interval with the service unavailable during installation or fixed reading; do not claim an atomic continuous switch. With a live mutable layer over an old representation, provide an explicit rule for current membership and conflicts and deletion markers so stale results disappear and opening uses the selected edition.
7. Separate historical coherence from current authorization. Before current use with significant consequences, check the governing source and active conditions; reconsider superseded advice. Failure of a vector update permits current exact, lexical, or direct search, or a confirmed unchanged subset, only with a stated scope. Rolling a service back to an old generation does not make an old rule current. Historical reading is permitted under retention rules and the reader's current permission.
8. Obtain the owner's meaning of deletion and revocation: distinguish removal from current membership, a withdrawn claim, retention expiry, and loss of reader access. Perform the required deletion or quarantine in indexes, caches, summaries, logs, and memory; if the supplier defers deletion, check current membership and permission when returning a result and actually reading the source. Pinning a generation does not preserve a revoked right. For immediate revocation, the source must actually enforce the restriction at operation time; state the remaining TOCTOU race rather than promising an instantaneous guarantee.

**Output and check.** A current, historical, or limited path with coherent identities of sources, representations, and reading and an honest readiness status. Test interrupted assembly before and after pointer switching, overlapping publishers and readers, saving and reloading, addition, changed conditions, parent relocation, splitting, deletion, permission loss, and failure of optional assembly. An open gen7 search is not mixed with opening gen8; expired old reading receives explicit unavailable. Trace affected dependencies in preserved judgments, compositions, handoffs, and memory; retain independently valid results. An unestablished dependency boundary requires broader inquiry, not declaring everything unaffected.

**Stop.** When the named operation can honestly work on a coherent basis or receives a precise limitation. A change in the source system, storage semantics, model, or authority reopens the relevant contract. Do not create update records merely to mark unchanged use; a historical edition and an event performed earlier are not rewritten by a new conclusion.

**Basis and limit.** KCAE.CHANGE:4.1–4.6; FPF A.10.1:4, G.11:4.2–4.3, A.6.3.RT:4.1, F.1:4.2/4.4. Concrete generation-publication, deletion, and reloading operations are a local specialization; storage guarantees require actual means.

**Worked application and changed conditions.** A complete constructed case. Current generation g7 relies on rule r7 and returns “retry is permissible.” The authorized source inventory receives r8, effective today: protocol v2 is needed; the installation record shows v1. Preserve r7/g7 for earlier reading, identify dependent advice and reading representations, and prepare g8 with exact r8 and a ready exact path. The optional vector representation has unavailable status: it does not withhold sufficient exact reading. After saving, closing, and loading, open r8 at its g8 address, check the v2 condition, and obtain the current conclusion “retry under this rule is not permitted”; the old historical r7 answer is not rewritten. Hand over g8 as current only through a supported installation means; without atomic protection, use agreed maintenance or fixed reading and state the limitation. Variation: a concurrent publisher has already installed g9. Do not publish stale g8 over g9; expected-previous-value protection rejects replacement, after which reconcile the new grounds. If source r8 is missing, the current conclusion depending on it is withheld while known history remains available. The example does not establish CAS, durability, or access revocation in a real store.

#### Current state after a material change

After a confirmed material state change and before handoff, find existing records used as current: a continuation pointer, short state description, startup instructions, or another actual entry for the next participant. Update only affected records or explicitly state the unknown and its boundary. Link the current claim to the subject/edition and latest confirmed observation; a new filename or later mtime does not by itself establish validity. If actual checking is unavailable, the earlier observation remains historical and the present state unknown.

Distinguish a current representation from historical evidence. For example, a service stopped after checking is later started by assignment: the current description should report the latest confirmed startup, while the earlier “stopped” report remains a report of its own session. Do not rewrite it as though the service was already running then. If two current entries contradict each other, check the actual state and grounds by authorized means; if reconciliation is impossible, explicitly hand over the conflict, withholding only the dependent action. Do not choose a convenient claim by reading order or extend local checking to unchecked parts. No new mandatory CURRENT file is required for every operation.

### KCA-05 v1.7 — Preserve conditional memory and questions that can be reopened

**When.** A relation between a source and work recurs, or an unresolved signal is needed for authorized subsequent use. **Input:** the original episode, dependencies on source, case, and criterion, storage purpose, access, and expiry. A one-off result without such use does not require memory.

**Basis and local addition.** FPF B.5.PI/A.16.1 preserves an early question without a premature answer; E.11.PUR and A.10.1/G.11 supply conditional reuse and reconsideration. The local addition is recording the original episode with dependencies on source and case, independent access outside the old label, reverse search by questions, and retention and deletion of every derived form. This permits return to the initial difficulty that a summary or former diagnosis may have hidden.

**Method.**
1. Preserve the smallest episode that permits reconsideration: original wording or observation, or an accessible protected link; recipient use; current interpretation; material rejected readings; open questions; and grounds for the conclusion. Observation and interpretation differ. An early signal is permissible without a diagnosis, selected method, or confidence: B.5.PI and, where needed, A.16.1 support its preservation, not proof of the final result.
2. Obtain purpose, access, and expiry from the owner within the assignment. Check that the protected link will actually be available later. If erased content may not be retained, a vector representation or paraphrase may also be a prohibited copy; apply deletion to all derived forms. Do not collect every conversation or expand the authorized corpus merely because a new method is useful.
3. Preserve the conditional relation: which source contribution helped which recipient result under what exact edition, case, criterion, and support conditions. A proposed work arrangement, assessment or recommendation, and observed successful application have different statuses. Reuse compares these prerequisites and the current question and currentness, not merely similarity; E.11.PUR:4.2.1 serves FPF pattern application. A missing dependency makes the old answer a reading candidate. Take a result directly from the part that remains valid; reopen a changed prerequisite through A.10.1/G.11 and direct domain checking.
4. For a bounded authorized store of open questions, let a new source or method search old missing results and conditions. Lexical and semantic reverse search produces candidates; then open the original episode and check the current case, source, and use. Preserve an independent route to the original episode outside the former label; sampled or targeted checking has a declared scope and cost, not a claim to reconsider every old case. An ended opportunity quietly ends the encounter with knowledge. Permission to remember is not permission to interrupt; check KCA-06 separately.
5. From significant omissions and false matches, propose a recognition question based on observable features, comparing matched cases with close but unsuitable ones. For example, “Does success require repeated manual compensation?” instead of only “Did a failure occur?” Check positive, negative, and unknown cases excluded from question development or constructed independently; using the same examples to derive and test a question does not produce independent evidence. If the question cannot be answered from the authorized projection, obtain permissible input or reject the aid. Neither a checklist nor a learned label is the exclusive admission condition for a future signal.
6. End a record's active validity or recognize its unsuitability when the source, case, authority, model, or opportunity changes. A new current rule does not refute a prior historical answer. An actual correction preserves grounds and the earlier observation; do not relabel the past episode under a new explanation. Periodically check whether retention is authorized and useful, derived copies, and net work savings against maintenance, false notifications, and disclosure. Narrow or delete memory that is too costly, preserving only legitimate historical grounds.

**Output, check, and stop.** A later reader recovers the original unresolved distinction, conditional relation, and exact grounds or exact unavailability. Test reaching an old question with new material outside the original label, a changed prerequisite with a surviving independent result, expiry and deletion of derived copies, and an unknown current case. A new source alone does not permit publishing an unsolicited current action for an old case. With a sufficient direct result, expired purpose, or net additional burden, stop, narrow, or delete memory.

**Example.** The label “Training issue” loses the episode “senior analyst removes duplicates after on-time export.” The preserved original observation and missing question about the protocol allow a new signed-configuration method to propose the needed fact retrieval. Before new advice, recheck the current case, source, and purpose; a completed client project remains a quiet historical result under permissible retention.

**Basis and limit.** KCAE.MEMORY:4.1–4.5; FPF B.5.PI:4.3–4.5, A.16.1:4, E.11.PUR:4.2.1, A.10.1:4, G.11:4.3, C.11.DUA:4. The concrete episode, reverse-question store, retention, and recognition cycle are a local method. Storage does not create permanent truth, permission, or an event observer.

**Worked application and changed conditions.** A complete constructed case. Original episode: “the export finishes on time; a senior analyst manually removes duplicates.” The old “training issue” label does not preserve the question about the duplicates' cause. With permission to retain it, preserve the original wording, date, case, source edition, and open question separately from the diagnosis. Later, a description of an idempotent-export method is found by reverse search for manual compensation; open the episode, check the present state and the necessary condition of a stable row ID. If the ID is unknown, a useful output is a question about the availability of an ID, not a ready implementation. Check: a reader without the old label recovers manual deduplication and the conditional next step. Variation: the project is completed or the purpose has expired—do not create a new request to the person; apply agreed retention or deletion; permission to remember does not permit interrupting the person. This is an explanatory example, not a memory-service test.

#### Continuation from preserved materials

When resuming, distinguish the current direct assignment and its conditions; used methods and editions; what was actually performed and the latest confirmed state; the open question and next authorized step. Reuse a sufficient existing record. A saved completed assignment, old plan, or proposal does not create a new task; reading an old record does not authorize repeating an external action. If the active assignment covers continuation, perform the nearest necessary step within its scope without renewed general agreement.

Reconcile current records in use under KCA-04 while retaining historical observations with their inputs. If a later event changed the state after a final report or packaging, state that boundary: an old archive and old observation need not include an event that had not yet occurred. When handing over to the next participant, make the subject/edition, current basis of authority, latest confirmed state, and next authorized step—or exact conflict/missing check—available. Do not infer current authority from a current label, textual similarity, or a date alone. Record scope is determined by dependent continuation; no log of every action is mandatory.

**Experience and checking limits.** Examples are constructed. The description has candidate status; actual suitability and results are checked for the specific inputs, performer, and conditions of application.

### KCA-06 v1.5 — Ensure an actual encounter with useful knowledge

**When.** There is an authorized situation in which a useful question or signal could enter ordinary work; nobody opens search because nobody yet sees the question. This is an optional method, not a standing mandate to observe. **Input:** authorized scope, purpose, recipient, time and retention, the actual situation, possible contribution, and execution-environment and recipient capabilities.

2. Obtain from each value holder comparisons of concrete consequence profiles: no worse, strict preference, indifference, or an open question. An open question is not equality. Distinguish end from means through “why?”/“how else?” questions and remove double counting of one consequence. Keep incompatible descriptions from different value holders separate. Combine them only under a legitimate aggregation or reconciliation rule.
3. Use coordinatewise dominance or a declared noncompensatory rule when sufficient. Numerical compensation is permissible only under a justified form. For minimized cost c and hours lost t, with constant increments and an exchange ratio over declared ranges, write `L_r=c+r*t`, `r>0`. Each x/y “no worse” comparison gives `c_x+r*t_x <= c_y+r*t_y`, strict preference gives a strict inequality, and indifference gives equality. Intersect all constraints on **one r**, preserving units and ranges. A unit change must preserve the comparison; normalized weights depend on ranges. Do not choose a convenient r without additional grounds.
4. If the affine assumption fails concrete comparisons at different initial levels, move to a supported rule/incomplete relation or an available nonlinear domain Method. For finite levels under a **justified additive form**, first establish whether at least one improvement is valued within the represented ranges. If the value holder is indifferent to every represented profile, return that relation directly without normalization. If it is unknown whether any distinction has value, preserve the exact gap and do not impose `sum(max q_i)=1`. Different pairwise supporting models do not establish this.

Cycles need a temporal boundary or an available dynamic model. Obtain a missing transition judgment from a competent source, checking feasibility with the supplier and the affected value-holder criterion.
7. Compare profiles under the same scenario and conditions. Preserve the stated horizon, shared resource, common cause, and duplicated effects. For every material uncertainty, find a boundary or reversal of the relation or a checkable range. #### Finite uncertain outcomes and incomplete risk preferences

Use this optional PSDA.6:4.5.6 branch only when the comparison needs preference between uncertain consequences and the value holder accepts an expected-utility form for the bounded use. A lottery is a hypothetical distribution over described consequences, which may be complete cost, delay and service profiles. A certain-outcome ordering alone does not determine risk preference: increasing transformations preserve that ordering but can reverse expected-utility comparisons.

Discuss the form before estimating utilities. Would mixing two compared lotteries with the same third lottery in the same proportions preserve their ordering? Test material cases, including loss of certainty. Would small probability changes permit small comparison changes, or must a strict priority or threshold prevail? Finite answers do not validate the form for every lottery. If common-mixture reversals, noncompensation or dependence on uncertainty presentation defeats this form, retain the expressed comparisons and obtain another suitable representation. Changing numbers alone cannot repair every failed form.

Enumerate the finite complete profiles in the alternatives and questions. Obtain common worst and best anchors w and b that the holder strictly distinguishes over this set; set `u(w)=0`, `u(b)=1`, with other profile utilities between 0 and 1. Unsupported anchors remain a gap; complete indifference returns that relation directly. For a profile x, compare certain x with a lottery of b with probability p and w otherwise. Indifference gives `u(x)=p`; x no worse gives `u(x)>=p`; the lottery no worse gives `u(x)<=p`. These hypothetical probabilities elicit preferences, not the intervention's actual chance of success.

Put every answer on the same utility variables. Lottery L no worse than M imposes `sum_x L(x)*u(x) >= sum_x M(x)*u(x)`; retain equality, strictness and unanswered comparisons distinctly. ### RES-INQUIRY v1.4 — Obtain a grounded answer open to substantive criticism and avoid commissioning unnecessary inquiry

**Preparation.** The recipient's question, intended use and deadline, available sources and current frame, proposed claim or knowledge change, serious competing explanations, access, ethics, safety, capability, and resources.

For traceability, retain only what criticism and recovery need: sources and editions, actual conduct, transformations, deviations, contributions, access, and losses. Mark a later reconstruction as such and preserve only earlier conduct or timing warranted by surviving evidence. Missing essential material limits the examination; a reconstructed protocol is not a prospective record, and a rerun is new work with its own design and trace. In analysis, select the operation for the specific claim; name the threat, what can expose it, the result actually checked, and its effect on the conclusion.
